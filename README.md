# Stint

**Time tracking for firms that bill by the hour — on your own office PC.**

Stint is a free, self-hosted time tracker for small engineering and consulting firms. One office
computer runs the Stint Server; everyone else installs the Stint app, picks the company from a
list, and logs in. No cloud, no subscriptions, no IT department required.

> **Status:** under active development — see [docs/PROGRESS.md](docs/PROGRESS.md) and
> [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md). The full user guide, screenshots and quick start arrive
> with the first release.
